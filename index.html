Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord bot ("the Bot") collects, uses, and protects information when you use or interact with the Bot on Discord.

2. Information We Collect

The Bot may process limited information provided by Discord, including:

  • Discord User ID
  • Discord Server (Guild) ID
  • Discord Channel ID
  • Discord Role ID
  • Discord username and display name
  • Information related to commands and interactions with the Bot

The Bot does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, private messages, or other sensitive personal information.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by the Bot may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the Bot's features
  • Manage server roles and permissions
  • Respond to commands and interactions
  • Prevent abuse and unauthorized use
  • Maintain security and stability
  • Diagnose technical problems and errors

4. Data Storage

The Bot only stores information that is necessary for its functionality. If information is stored, it is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's services or maintain security.

We do not sell or rent user information to third parties.

5. Discord

The Bot operates through Discord and is subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

6. Third-Party Services

The Bot may use third-party services required for hosting, databases, logging, or other technical functionality.

7. Data Deletion

If you believe the Bot has stored information associated with you and you would like that information removed, you may contact the Bot's owner or server administration.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by the Bot. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not specifically directed toward children under the minimum age required to use Discord.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When changes are made, the "Last updated" date will be updated.

11. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Bot's owner or server administration through the Discord server where the Bot is used.
